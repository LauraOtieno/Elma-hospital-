🎯 Overview
ELMA Healthcare eliminates fragmented patient records, reduces diagnostic delays, and improves healthcare delivery through AI-powered clinical insights and integrated patient management. Built for hospitals, clinics, and health systems across Sub-Saharan Africa.
The Problem We Solve

❌ Fragmented medical records → inconsistent care
❌ Manual patient intake → long wait times
❌ Limited specialist access → delayed diagnoses
❌ Poor data visibility → inefficient resource allocation
❌ Offline operations → data loss and duplication

Our Solution

✅ Unified electronic health records (EHR)
✅ Smart clinical decision support
✅ Telemedicine for specialist access
✅ Real-time patient dashboards


⚡ Features
Clinical Management

Electronic Health Records (EHR) - Comprehensive patient histories, diagnoses, treatments, medications
Patient Registration - Fast onboarding with biometric identification support
Appointment Scheduling - In-person and telemedicine consultations
Clinical Notes - SOAP documentation, vital signs tracking, medical imaging integration
Prescription Management - Digital prescriptions with pharmacy integration
